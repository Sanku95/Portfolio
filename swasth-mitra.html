Swasth Mitra

A healthcare product concept designed to bring essential services and support into one accessible mobile experience.

Mobile appProduct strategyUX/UI

01 / The opportunity

Make healthcare services easier to access

The concept brings several healthcare needs together in one place, helping people discover services and navigate support without jumping between disconnected experiences.

02 / Product scope

Healthcare services

Explore pharmacy, pathology, and healthcare vendors through a unified experience.

Community support

Support membership and fundraising journeys designed around clarity and trust.

Wallet experience

Bring relevant payment and wallet tasks into a consistent mobile flow.

Connected journeys

Organize multiple service flows into a coherent product structure.

03 / Design focus

Reduce complexity across workflows

The design direction prioritizes clear navigation, understandable steps, and consistent patterns across membership, fundraising, wallet, and service-discovery journeys.
