The challenge

A messy feedback process

Meet Nabil, a product manager who oversees design, development and testing teams. For him, feedback was slow and messy. He took screenshots by hand, annotated them, and sent them over WhatsApp or email. Tasks got lost in long chats.

There was no central place for feedback, and no tool that let him comment inside mobile apps. He soon realized that other product managers, designers and developers had the same problem.

  • Manual screenshots. Capturing and marking up every issue by hand.
  • Scattered channels. Feedback spread across WhatsApp and email.
  • Lost tasks. Action items buried in long conversations.
  • No mobile app commenting. Nowhere to leave feedback inside an app.

The solution

Comment right on the thing itself

Bugsmash lets people comment directly on media, including apps. Teams annotate issues visually, keep all feedback in one place, and track every task through to done.

Visual annotation

Point at the exact spot and say what needs to change.

Centralized feedback

One home for comments instead of chats and inboxes.

Task tracking

Turn comments into tasks that nobody loses.

Real-time updates

Everyone sees changes as they happen.

Actionable workflows

Move from feedback to fixes without leaving the tool.
