Expense Tracker

An offline-first personal finance app designed to help people track spending while keeping their financial data on their own device.

Product designPrivacy-firstAndroid

01 / The problem

Make expense tracking practical and private

The product focuses on everyday expense management without requiring users to send their financial information to a remote service.

02 / Core capabilities

Offline by design

Expense data is stored locally on the device, with no data sharing built into the app.

Backup and recovery

A rolling backup approach helps users preserve their records, with JSON backup support.

Export when needed

Export records to PDF or Excel for review and personal record keeping.

Everyday budgeting

Support personal expense tracking and budget awareness through a focused utility experience.

03 / Product principles

Utility without unnecessary complexity

The product direction prioritizes privacy, dependable local storage, and straightforward access to personal records.
