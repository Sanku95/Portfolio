OKUS Tea

A tea brand identity built around a memorable wordmark, considered details, and a premium yet approachable packaging direction.

Brand strategyVisual identityPackaging

01 / The idea

A distinctive identity for everyday tea

OKUS means “taste” or “flavour” in Croatian. The identity aims to connect the ritual of tea with a refined, recognizable brand system.

02 / Visual direction

Wordmark

A serif “OKUS” wordmark with a leaf detail integrated into the O, supported by flowing lines and a centered TEA descriptor.

Color palette

Deep green for heritage and nature, paired with warm gold accents for a premium feel.

Packaging system

A clean white base with clear product naming and a consistent layout that can extend across tea varieties and pack sizes.

Product range

Planned range includes Royal Assam Tea, Classic Assam Tea, and Gold Assam Tea.

03 / Design rationale

Build consistency across every pack

The design direction balances a distinctive brand mark with legible product information so the identity can scale across different pack sizes and tea variants.
